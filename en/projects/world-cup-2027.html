About the project

A competition that unites the world’s strongest freedivers

The WDC World Cup is held under the unified international rules of the Sports Committee. Athletes compete in depth disciplines in open water and in the pool, and results go straight into the world ranking.

Each stage is more than a competition: open training sessions, seminars for coaches and judges, medical support and a programme for spectators.

  • Depth disciplines: CWT, CWTB, FIM, CNF
  • Pool: dynamic with and without fins
  • Individual and national team rankings

Ask a question
Who can take part in the World Cup?

Athletes aged 18+ who are part of a national team or a club belonging to a National Dive League. A medical clearance and a confirmed result in the discipline are required.

How does a national team apply?

Click “Take part” and leave your contact details — we’ll send you the rules and the registration form. The team entry is submitted by a National Dive League representative by 1 February 2027.

Can I compete at just one stage?

Yes. Ranking points are awarded for each stage, and the best athletes by the total of three stages qualify for the final.

How much does it cost to take part?

The entry fee depends on the stage and the number of disciplines. Current terms are in the competition rules we’ll send after your request.

How is safety ensured?

Safety divers, doctors and WDC-certified judges work at every stage. All dives follow unified safety protocols.

Can I come as a spectator?

Yes, entry to the spectator areas is free. The final in Dahab has a guest programme and live broadcasts.
